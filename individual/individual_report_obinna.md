# Reflective Report

This project helped me improve both my technical skills and my ability to work with other people. My main contributions were in **data cleaning**, supporting **some parts of the exploratory data analysis**, and keeping records of our individual team meetings. I helped the team prepare the datasets so that the data could be easier to understand, clean, and analyse.

## Technical Skills Developed

One important skill I developed was **data cleaning**. The datasets were not easy to work with because some parts had missing values, unclear columns, duplicates, and inconsistent formatting. This made me understand that good analysis cannot happen without clean and well-structured data.

I learned that before building models or making conclusions, it is important to first check the quality of the data carefully. This helped me improve my attention to detail and made me more confident in handling real-world messy datasets.

## Contribution to EDA

I also made a small contribution to the **exploratory data analysis** stage. I helped with some basic checks and supported the team in understanding parts of the data. This gave me a better understanding of how EDA helps a project before deeper analysis is done.

I learned that EDA is useful because it helps the team identify patterns, possible errors, and important variables in the dataset.

## Teamwork and Communication

The project also helped me improve my teamwork and communication skills. As an introvert, I sometimes find it difficult to reach out to others or speak openly in a group. However, this project taught me that teamwork requires regular communication.

I learned that when I am unsure about something, it is better to ask my team members for help instead of struggling alone. This improved my confidence and helped me become more comfortable working with others.

## Meeting Records and Organisation

I also helped in keeping records of our individual team meetings. This improved my organisation skills because I had to note key discussions, decisions and progress made by the team.

Keeping records helped the group remember what had been agreed and what tasks still needed to be completed. This showed me the importance of documentation in project work.

## Areas for Improvement

However, I still have areas to improve. I still find public speaking difficult, especially when I have to present in front of people. Even though I am not fully confident yet, this project helped me improve because I had to communicate more with my group and explain some of my work.

In future projects, I want to keep improving my confidence in speaking, presenting, and sharing my ideas clearly.

## Conclusion

Overall, this project improved me in many areas. It helped me build stronger technical skills in data cleaning and gave me a better understanding of exploratory data analysis. It also helped me improve my teamwork, communication, and organisation skills.

Most importantly, it taught me that successful project work is not only about technical knowledge, but also about working well with others, asking for help when needed, and keeping clear records of team progress.
