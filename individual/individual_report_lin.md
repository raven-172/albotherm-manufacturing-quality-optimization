# Reflective Report

On individual-level, this project gave me the opportunity to work together across disciplines as an MSc Fintech student alongside MSc Data Science colleagues. Even though I had limited technical experience at the time the project started, I was able to develop my technical skills as I worked through the project. This project also complemented my other modules by allowing me to learn or apply skills and knowledge of those other modules in a practical setting. 

I also got to develop my communication and organisational skills by acting as a liaison between our team and Albotherm. I managed email correspondence and ensured clear communication between both parties, allowing me to contribute effectively in a technical setting. I also supported team coordination by preparing the Data Security Agreement and ensuring it is signed by both parties. Additionally, I was able to develop my organisational skills by contributing to meeting notes and research on optimisation.

The project also allowed me to develop my critical thinking skills. For instance, by working on the data preparation process, I was able to recognise issues in the initial cleaning and merging stages and suggest revisiting the dataset preprocessing. This improved the overall reliability of our analysis and showed me the importance of questioning early-stage outputs.

In terms of areas for improvement, I learned that I should speak up more confidently. At the beginning, I was hesitant to share my ideas due to my limited technical background. However, through peer support and feedback, I became more comfortable contributing to discussions. Speaking up confidently proved valuable both for my own learning and for the team’s progress.

Overall, this experience enabled me to develop both technical and soft skills. I am pleased with the progress I made while working on a collaborative project with real-life impact.
